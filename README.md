
# Retail Sales & Profit Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview

This project is an end-to-end Data Analytics project using the Superstore retail dataset.

The objective is to analyze sales, profitability, customers, products, regions, discounts, shipping performance, and business segments to identify important business trends and opportunities.

## 🎯 Business Objectives

- Analyze overall sales and profit performance
- Identify the most profitable product categories
- Find top-performing products and customers
- Identify loss-making products
- Compare regional performance
- Analyze customer segments
- Understand the relationship between discount and profit
- Analyze shipping performance
- Build an interactive Power BI dashboard
- Generate actionable business recommendations

- Category-level sales and profitability

- Shipping efficiency

## 📈 Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis, businesses can:

1. Review products with high sales but low or negative profit.
2. Optimize discount strategies for weak-margin products.
3. Focus on high-value and profitable customers.
4. Improve performance in weaker regions and sub-categories.
5. Evaluate shipping methods based on cost, speed, and profitability.
6. Prioritize products and customer groups that provide sustainable profit.
