# Retail Sales & Profit Analysis

## 📊 Project Overview

An end-to-end Data Analytics project analyzing retail sales and profitability using Python, SQL, and Power BI.

The project focuses on identifying sales trends, profitable and loss-making products, customer performance, regional performance, discount impact, shipping efficiency, and business opportunities.

---

## 🎯 Business Objectives

- Analyze overall sales and profit performance
- Identify high-performing product categories
- Find top products and customers
- Identify loss-making products
- Compare regional performance
- Analyze customer segments
- Understand the impact of discounts on profit
- Evaluate shipping performance
- Build an interactive business intelligence dashboard
- Generate actionable business recommendations
